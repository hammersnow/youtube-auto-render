### Why this is new vs V7/recent signals
Existing V7/research covers wrong-motion, maze/tracking, spot-difference, physics-error, portal/laser, `predict_path`, `chain_reaction_choice`, and ambiguous `direction_lock`. `hidden_mechanism` asks a different question: **what unseen mechanism explains the visible motion?** The payoff is an x-ray/cutaway reveal, not merely the final destination, causal chain, or motion direction.

### Original mechanic
1. 0.0–0.8s: immediately show 5–8 visible dots/tiles moving in a surprising but deterministic pattern; hook: `WHAT'S MOVING THEM? A / B / C`.
2. 0.8–5.5s: maintain uninterrupted satisfying motion while three tiny schematic mechanism choices remain visible (e.g. hidden wheel, belt/cam, magnetic orbit). Avoid copied physical designs; generate abstract geometry procedurally.
3. 5.5–7.5s: freeze for a very short commitment beat, then fade the cover plate to an x-ray/cutaway view.
4. 7.5–10.0s: show the correct procedural mechanism driving exactly the same trajectories; add a clean pulse on the matching choice and loop back to the covered state.

### Expected benefit
- Adds a genuine curiosity gap before reveal: viewers need the hidden explanation, not just the answer location.
- Combines puzzle participation with satisfying mechanical motion and a second visual payoff (cutaway reveal), giving replay value because the viewer can rewatch the visible motion after learning the mechanism.
- Strongly differentiates from current V7 families without paid assets or generative-video APIs.

### Free implementation path
Python + NumPy/OpenCV/Pillow only. Generate deterministic point trajectories from a hidden parametric mechanism (eccentric wheel/cam, belt loop, linked crank). Render the first phase with the mechanism masked by a glass/metal panel; reveal by alpha-fading that mask and drawing linkage paths. FFmpeg handles H.264/AAC as today. No external footage/assets required.
