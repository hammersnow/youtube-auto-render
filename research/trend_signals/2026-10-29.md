# Trend signal — 2026-10-29

## Signal: `satisfaction_choice`

A subjective-choice mechanic is showing a useful current signal: show 3–4 short, visually distinct micro-events and ask **WHICH ONE IS MOST SATISFYING?** before/while they play. Unlike a normal puzzle, there is no canonical correct answer; the payoff is repeated visual completion plus an A/B/C preference choice that can drive comments without withholding a solution.

## Evidence

- Power Hot Ball repeatedly published the exact “Which Item is Most Satisfying?” framing during October. The Oct 23 Short reached ~885.6K views, while nearby uploads using the same framing reached ~277K (Oct 22), ~54K (Oct 26), ~30.6K (Oct 24) and ~118K (Oct 10). The repeated use plus one large current breakout is a stronger signal than a one-off title. Source (stats captured Oct 28): https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/%40powerhotball/videos/
- The same channel alternates ~10–11 s single-payoff clips with ~1:00 multi-item choice videos, suggesting the choice wrapper is being used as a retention structure rather than only an object/ASMR theme.
- Platform-level context still favors very fast comprehension: YouTube’s Shorts guidance emphasizes the first-second hook, and 2026 Metricool data reports average Shorts view duration around 16 s even as views increased sharply. Sources: https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/youtube-shorts-deep-dive/ and https://metricool.com/youtube-trends/

## Why it is new vs V7

Current V7 proven families are `wrong_motion`, `animated_maze`, `memory_shuffle`, `spot_difference`; experiments are `physics_error`, `portal_path`, `laser_core`, `flow_field`.

`satisfaction_choice` is not another path/error/difference puzzle. It changes the interaction contract from **find the correct answer** to **rank several satisfying outcomes**. That gives V7 a comment-oriented, answer-independent family while keeping the procedural visual language.

## Original implementation idea

Generate 3 deterministic 2.5–3.5 s micro-scenes labeled A/B/C, e.g.:

- particles snapping perfectly into a silhouette;
- colored balls sorting through gates into matching bins;
- a geometric chain collapsing into an exact packed grid.

Hook on frame 1: `WHICH ONE IS MOST SATISFYING? A / B / C`.

Sequence A → B → C with no dead air, then finish on a 1–1.5 s three-panel freeze: `A, B OR C?`. Keep every scene original/procedural; do not imitate the source objects, hot-ball experiment, camera setup, or audio.

Implementation can stay fully free/open-source: NumPy for deterministic state, Pillow/OpenCV for geometry/particles/compositing, FFmpeg for assembly/audio. No paid generator is needed.

## Expected benefit

- Three payoff moments per Short instead of one reveal.
- First-frame question is instantly readable and survives sound-off viewing.
- Subjective answer avoids trivial/repeated solution fatigue and creates a natural comment prompt.
- Can reuse V7 rendering primitives while remaining mechanically distinct from existing families.
