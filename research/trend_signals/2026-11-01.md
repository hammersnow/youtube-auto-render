### V7 gap
Current V7 families are `wrong_motion`, `signal_core_v2`, `memory_shuffle`, `spot_difference_v2`, `physics_error`, `portal_path_v2`, `laser_core`, and `flow_field`. None directly tests **reaction timing under a shrinking temporal window**. This is distinct from tracking/memory: the viewer predicts when/where to act against a moving hazard or cue, with difficulty controlled primarily by time-to-contact.

### Originalizable concept
`reaction_timing`: show a target/token and a moving hazard or cue. Hook: **MOVE NOW OR WAIT?** / **TAP AT THE PERFECT MOMENT**. Run 2–3 escalating rounds in ~10–15s. The safe timing window visibly shrinks each round. Freeze-frame the collision/contact boundary for a 0.5–1.0s reveal, then loop cleanly into the next attempt.

Do not copy creator scenes, props, sports, people, or choreography. Use procedural geometric targets, gates, sweepers, pulses, orbiting hazards, or moving bars.

### Free implementation
- NumPy: deterministic trajectories and exact time-to-contact/window calculation.
- OpenCV/Pillow: target/hazard rendering, countdown ring, impact/freeze overlays.
- FFmpeg: speed ramps, impact sound alignment, seamless loop.
- No paid assets or models required.

### Expected benefit
Adds a time-domain puzzle family to V7 instead of another spatial-detection variant. It naturally creates escalating tension, an immediate first-frame objective, replay incentive (viewers mentally retry the timing), and deterministic answer validation.
