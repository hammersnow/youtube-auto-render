## Proposed V7 experiment: `predict_path`
Show a ball/particle at frame 1, a compact obstacle field, and 3 labelled endpoints. Hook: **WHERE WILL IT LAND?** Viewer commits to A/B/C before motion begins. After ~0.8 s, the ball launches and follows a deterministic collision path; reveal lands directly on the answer with a short shockwave/chime. Loop by returning the end-state composition to the opening geometry.

Why it is meaningfully different from current V7:
- `animated_maze`: route-solving/navigation.
- `physics_error`: identify an impossible/wrong physical event.
- `laser_core`: beam/path spectacle.
- `predict_path`: viewer predicts the endpoint *before* a satisfying continuous-motion payoff.

## Free implementation
Python + NumPy/OpenCV only:
- analytic circle/segment collision or simple deterministic ray reflection;
- precompute the path, then render with V7 motion trails/bloom;
- 3 endpoint gates with large safe-area labels;
- subtle tick during prediction window, collision clicks, existing reveal chime;
- no external assets or paid API.

## Expected benefit
Combines an explicit participation hook (A/B/C prediction) with uninterrupted satisfying motion, giving the experimental slot a stronger reason to watch through the reveal while remaining fully procedural and cheap to render.
